What the real app can and can't do

  • Every screen is a built-in CarPlay template: CPTabBarTemplate, CPListTemplate, CPPointOfInterestTemplate, CPActionSheetTemplate, CPInformationTemplate. CarPlay draws them in the system style, so the final look comes from Apple, not this page.
  • There's no destination to enter. GPS measures the miles, and the start and end addresses come from where the trip starts and ends. The end address is the destination in the mileage log.
  • On the trip map, the app updates the pins and the card text about every 5 seconds. The faint trail and smooth motion here are only for the mockup. A Driving task app can't draw a route line or a map that follows the car.
  • Siri can start and end a trip hands-free: “Start a trip in Skyway” and “End my trip in Skyway”. Siri then asks Business or Personal.
  • The deduction is shown as an estimate while driving. It counts only if you save the trip as Business; Personal trips keep the miles with $0.
  • Renaming the destination, changing Business or Personal, or adding a note happens later on the iPhone.
  • The other tabs (Flight, Airport, Checklist) are the existing Skyway CarPlay screens and stay unchanged.
