What the real app can and can't do

  • Every screen is a built-in CarPlay template: CPTabBarTemplate, CPListTemplate, CPPointOfInterestTemplate, CPActionSheetTemplate, CPInformationTemplate. CarPlay draws them in the system style, so the final look comes from Apple, not this page.
  • On the trip map, the app updates the pins and the card text about every 5 seconds. The faint trail and smooth motion here are only for the mockup. A Driving task app can't draw a route line or a map that follows the car; that needs the Navigation entitlement.
  • The destination comes from this list or from Siri ("Start a trip to Office in Skyway"). Driving task apps can't show a keyboard or take dictation.
  • The deduction is shown as an estimate while driving. It counts only if you save the trip as Business; Personal trips keep the miles with $0.
  • The other tabs (Flight, Airport, Checklist) are the existing Skyway CarPlay screens and stay unchanged.
