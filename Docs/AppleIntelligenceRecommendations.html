What Skyway has today

Siri phrases

GetCurrentTripIntent, StartDriveTripIntent and EndDriveTripIntent, with phrases in SkywayShortcuts.swift.

Onscreen awareness

TripEntity (indexed in Spotlight) and TravelActionEntity, through .userActivity and .appEntityIdentifier.

In-app assistant

FlightAssistant with the context lens. Its answers come from hard-coded keyword matching. It doesn't use a model yet.

Constraint: there's no travel schema domain

The Apple Intelligence schema domains are audio, calendar, camera, clock, files, mail, maps, messages, notes, phone, photos, reminders and system/search. None of them covers travel or mileage, so Siri reaches Skyway through plain App Intents, App Shortcuts, entities and onscreen annotations. Forcing a schema that doesn't fit makes Siri worse.

C. Shortcuts automations & system features

CarPlay connects → Start a Trip

…and End Trip when it disconnects. Needs A8, plus A2 or B5 for the purpose.

Arrive at office → End as Business

A location automation that passes the purpose parameter.

Morning of the flight → Leave-by alert

A time-of-day automation that uses the new leave-by intent (A5).

Control Center & Action Button

A ControlWidgetButton to Start or End a trip with the existing intents.

Live Activity

Shows the drive in progress (miles, End button) and boarding (gate, countdown).
